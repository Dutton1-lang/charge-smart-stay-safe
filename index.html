Lithium Battery Safety

Safe Use • Safe Charging • Safe Disposal

Protect your home from lithium battery fires

Lithium-ion batteries power many everyday devices including phones, e-bikes, e-scooters, laptops and power tools. When used safely they are extremely reliable. However, damaged batteries, poor quality chargers or unsafe charging practices can cause fires that develop extremely quickly. Follow our simple guidance to help keep you, your family and your home safe.

📱

Phones & Tablets

Charge safely using the manufacturer's charger.

🚲

E-Bikes

Never charge on escape routes or overnight.

🛴

E-Scooters

Use approved batteries and chargers only.

🔋

Power Banks

Replace damaged batteries immediately.

🛠

Power Tools

Store and charge correctly.

♻️

Battery Disposal

Never place lithium batteries in household bins.

Charge Smart. Stay Safe.

Small changes can prevent devastating fires. Always use the correct charger, never leave batteries charging unattended, and dispose of damaged batteries safely.
